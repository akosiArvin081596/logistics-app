--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

When you pull out of the shipper loaded, the app notices you've left and moves
you to In Transit automatically. Same again when you reach the receiver.

This is why keeping the truck's ELD powered and connected matters more than any
button in this app. The tracking, your status, and the customer's view of where
their freight is all run off it.

If you ever notice the status stuck on something that's clearly wrong, that's
worth a message to dispatch — it usually means the truck stopped reporting.
