--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

Welcome to LogisX — the app you'll use for every load you run for us.

Everything starts here. Open the app and you'll see this sign-in screen.

Your username isn't your name — it's a driver ID that dispatch gives you when
you're set up. On most accounts it looks like "LogisX", a dash, and four numbers.
Whatever yours is, it doesn't change, so it's worth saving in your phone.

Type your username, type your password, and tap Sign In.

If you've forgotten your password, don't guess at it — call dispatch and
they'll reset it for you.

--- ON-SCREEN CUES ---

- Hold on the full sign-in screen for the first two sentences.
- Highlight the username field as the driver ID is explained.
- Highlight the password field, then the Sign In button.

--- ACCURACY NOTE ---

Every driver account checked used the LogisX-#### shape, but that was read off
existing accounts, not off the onboarding process. The line is hedged ("on most
accounts") so it stays true if dispatch ever issues a different format. Tighten
it only if you know the format is guaranteed.
