SCREEN     : Fuel — where to fill up

--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

Scroll on and the app answers the next question: where.

These are diesel stops along the route you're already driving, with live pump
prices, cheapest first. The green tag marks the best one.

Each stop gives you the brand, the address, how old that price is, and how far
it sits off your route — usually only a couple of tenths of a mile.

Tap Navigate on any of them and it routes you straight there.

Take thirty seconds over this before you pull in somewhere. On a full tank, a
few cents a gallon is real money, and the cheaper stop is often barely off your
route.

--- ON-SCREEN CUES ---

- Highlight the section heading, then the CHEAPEST stop card.
- Compare the two prices side by side.
- Point out "off route" distance, then Navigate.
