--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

When dispatch sends you a load, it lands in Pending — and the tab shows a number
so you know something's waiting.

Notice you can have one load running and another waiting here at the same time.
That's normal — dispatch lines your next one up early so you can plan your
hours. You still run them one at a time.

There are two ways to answer it. Accept and Decline sit right on the card, so
you can respond without opening anything.

But open it first. Tap the card, read the pickup and drop-off details, and check
the appointment times against your hours. The same two buttons are waiting at the
bottom of the load — Accept Load takes it and it becomes yours, Decline Load
sends it back to dispatch so they can give it to someone else.

Be straight with us here. If the load doesn't work — you haven't got the hours,
or you can't make the appointment — decline it and tell dispatch why. That's far
better than accepting it and running late.

--- ON-SCREEN CUES ---

- Highlight the Pending tab and its count badge.
- Scroll down through the load to reach the buttons.
- Hold on Decline Load and Accept Load while both are explained.

--- PRODUCTION NOTE ---

The buttons sit at the very bottom of the load — show the scroll, or drivers will look for them at the top.
