--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

Last tab: Invoices. This is your pay.

At the top is the pay week — it runs Saturday to Friday — and you can step back
through previous weeks with the arrows.

Generate Weekly Invoice builds this week's invoice from the loads you've
delivered. You usually won't need it — the invoice is generated for you
automatically every Friday evening.

What does matter is the cut-off: six thirty on Friday evening, Central time.
Anything delivered and closed out before then lands in that week's pay. Miss it,
and the load rolls into next week. So if you deliver late on a Friday, close the
load out — don't leave it for the morning.

Underneath is your invoice history. Each one shows the invoice number, the week
it covers, what you were paid, how many loads it covered, and the receipts you
filed against it.

The status tells you where it is: Draft means it hasn't been submitted, Submitted
means it's with the office, and Approved means it's been signed off.

Check this every week. If a load you delivered isn't on there, tell dispatch
straight away — the sooner you spot it, the easier it is to fix.

--- ON-SCREEN CUES ---

- Highlight the week selector and the back arrow.
- Hold on Generate Weekly Invoice.

- Highlight Draft, Submitted and Approved in the history list.
