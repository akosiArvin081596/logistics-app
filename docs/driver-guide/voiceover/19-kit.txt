--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

Kit is where your paperwork lives, in three groups.

My Identity Documents is your own — your CDL, front and back, and your medical
card. Check the expiry dates here. If your medical card is close to running out,
sort it before it lapses, not after.

Truck Documents is the paperwork for the truck you're assigned to — registration,
insurance and permits. If you're stopped at a scale and asked for them, this is
where you get them.

Shared Documents is anything the office has sent you specifically.

It's worth opening Kit once before a long run so you know what's there.
