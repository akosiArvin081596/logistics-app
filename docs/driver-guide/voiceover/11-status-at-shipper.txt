--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

Now the part most drivers get wrong, so listen closely: you do not have to
update your status.

The six circles are your load, start to finish. Heading to Shipper, At Shipper,
Loading, In Transit, At Receiver, Delivered.

Your truck moves these on its own. The ELD in the cab reports where you are, and
when you pull into the shipper the app sees it and marks you At Shipper by
itself. You don't tap anything. You don't call it in.

The button underneath is a backup, nothing more. If you've arrived and the app
hasn't caught up — bad signal, or the yard sits outside the mapped zone — you
can tap it to move things along yourself. Most days you won't need to.

So arrive, do your job, and let the truck do the paperwork.

--- ON-SCREEN CUES ---

- Walk along the six circles as they're named.
- Emphasise the tick appearing WITHOUT anyone tapping.
- Point at the blue button only when it's called a backup — don't dwell on it.
