--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

Messages is your direct line to dispatch, and it keeps the whole conversation
attached to the app rather than scattered across personal phones.

There's a General channel, and you can message about a specific load from the
load itself — which is better, because dispatch can see exactly which load you
mean without having to ask.

Use this for anything that isn't urgent: a delay, a question about an address,
a problem at the dock.

If it's an emergency, or the truck isn't safe to move — phone. Don't type it.
