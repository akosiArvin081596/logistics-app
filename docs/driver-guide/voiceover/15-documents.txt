--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

Open the Documents section and pick what you're uploading — Proof of Delivery,
Bill of Lading, a receipt, or anything else.

For a POD or a BOL, use Scan Document. Scan straightens the page, crops the
edges and cleans up the lighting, so it comes out like a proper scan instead of
a photo of a bit of paper on your steering wheel. Brokers reject bad photos, and
a rejected POD delays everyone's money.

If the paperwork runs to more than one page, scan the first page, then use the
plus button to add the next.

Take Photo and Upload File are there for anything that isn't a POD or BOL.

Once it's uploaded you'll see it listed with a thumbnail — tap it to check it's
straight and readable before you drive off.

And now the Delivered button will be waiting for you back up in Update Status.
