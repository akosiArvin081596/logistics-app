--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

Once you're in, this is your home screen: your Loads.

Look at the three tabs across the top. Active is the load you're running right
now. Pending is a load dispatch has sent you that you haven't answered yet.
Historical is everything you've already delivered — it stays here so you can
look back at any load you've run.

Each load shows up as a card. The number at the top is the Load ID — that's the
number dispatch will use when they call you, so it's the one to quote.

On the right is your current status. Underneath, the pickup city and the
delivery city, and the dates for each.

Tap anywhere on the card to open the load.

Along the bottom you've got five buttons: Loads, Alerts, Kit, Messages and
Invoices. We'll go through each one.

--- ON-SCREEN CUES ---

- Highlight Active / Pending / Historical in turn.
- Circle the Load ID at the top-left of the card.
- Circle the status pill on the right.
- Point to the pickup-to-delivery line and the two dates.
- Sweep across the five bottom-bar buttons at the end.
