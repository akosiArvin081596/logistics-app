--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

First section: the Route Map.

Across the top you get the three numbers that matter. Total miles for the trip.
Your estimated driving time. And the distance from where your truck is right now
to your next stop — that's the amber one.

Underneath that is your estimated arrival time.

The blue dot is your truck. That position comes from the ELD in the cab, so it
updates on its own — you don't have to do anything, and you don't need to share
your phone's location.

The green marker is your next stop.

Tap Navigate and it hands off to your maps app for turn-by-turn directions.

You can switch between Map and Hybrid view, and Route Options underneath will
show you alternative routes if there's more than one sensible way to run it.

--- ON-SCREEN CUES ---

- Highlight each of the three figures in turn: miles, ETA, distance to stop.
- Point to the blue dot, then the green marker.
- Hold on the Navigate button.
- Tap Map / Hybrid to show the difference.

--- PRODUCTION NOTE ---

Say the ELD line clearly — drivers regularly assume tracking needs their phone. It does not.
