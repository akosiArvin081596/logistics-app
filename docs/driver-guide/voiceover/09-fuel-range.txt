--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

The Fuel section is one of the most useful screens in the app, and it runs
entirely off your truck — you don't enter anything.

The big number is the one to read: miles before you need to refuel. Underneath
it tells you how far you'd get before the tank is actually dry, and how much
it's holding back as a reserve. So the headline figure is miles you can use, not
the last drop.

Above it, the app compares that against the run you're on. Here it's saying the
range is too close to the miles still to drive, so plan a stop — and it names
the mileage to fuel up within.

The bar shows three numbers: what you can count on, what a typical run gets, and
the best this truck has ever managed on this much fuel.

And look where those come from — your fuel level is read straight off the truck,
and the miles per gallon is measured from your own fill-ups. This isn't a guess
from a spreadsheet. It's your truck and your receipts.

--- ON-SCREEN CUES ---

- Highlight the big mileage figure first and hold on it.
- Then the verdict banner above, naming the fuel-up mileage.
- Trace the three-part bar: count on / typical / best.
- Finish on the "from your truck" and "from your fuel receipts" tags.
