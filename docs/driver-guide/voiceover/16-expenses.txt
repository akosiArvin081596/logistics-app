--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

The Expenses section is where you log anything you've paid for on this load.

Pick the Type — Fuel, Repair, Maintenance, Wear and Tear, Toll, Food or Other.
Enter the amount and the date.

Take a photo of the receipt and the app will read it for you. It fills in the
merchant, the city and the state on its own. Always check what it's filled in —
it's very good, but it isn't perfect, and you can correct any field.

If it's fuel, you'll also get Gallons and Odometer. Fill those in. That's what
lets us track your truck's fuel economy properly.

Then tap Log Expense.

Do this as you go. A receipt photographed at the pump takes ten seconds; a
shoebox of faded receipts at the end of the month is a nightmare for everyone —
and a receipt we can't read is one we may not be able to reimburse.
