--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

The Alerts tab is where notifications from dispatch land — a new load assigned to
you, a change to one you're running, or anything else that needs your attention.

The red badge on the bell tells you how many you haven't read. Tap any alert to
jump straight to the load it's about, and use Mark all read to clear them down.

Check this at the start of every shift.

- Highlight the red count badge on the Alerts icon.
- Tap an alert to show it opening the related load.
- Point out Mark all read.
