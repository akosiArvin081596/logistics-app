--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

The Kit tab is your paperwork, and it is the one to remember at a scale or an inspection.

At the top is you — your name, your carrier, your truck and how we reach you.

Scroll down and you get Truck Documents — the registration, the insurance and the IFTA papers for the truck you are in. Tap View, and show them straight off your phone.

Keep going and you will find your own licence and medical card, and every contract you have signed with us, so you always have a copy on you.

And anything the office has shared with you sits at the bottom. If your truck changes, all of this changes with it, so you are always showing the right papers.
