--- VOICE-OVER SCRIPT ---

Bought fuel, paid a toll, or had a repair on the road? Log it against the load while you're still standing there — open the load and tap Expenses.

Pick the type, take a picture of the receipt, and the app reads it for you. The amount, the date, the store, the city and the state all fill themselves in.

Always check what it filled in. It's very good, but it isn't perfect, and you can correct any field before you submit.

Then tap Submit Expense. If you accidentally log the same receipt twice, the app catches it and asks — it won't quietly double up.
