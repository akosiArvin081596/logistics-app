Platform Update — Mileage Tracking & Fuel Range

27 August 2026  ·  Live in production

Two items you raised on 26 August are built and live, plus one data error found while building them. Everything below is already running — nothing here is waiting on you, except the questions on the last page.

1Mileage tracking
Issue
No way to see how many miles each truck or driver runs per week, per month, or in total, and no company average.
Assessment
The platform had no driven-mileage reporting at all. The "Total Miles" figure on the Financials page is the quoted length of booked routes, not odometer distance — it misses deadhead, detours and repositioning, so it was never the number you were asking for.
Fix
A new Mileage Analytics page in the sidebar, for Admin and Dispatch. Miles per truck and per driver, broken out by week, month and total, plus the company average. Weeks run Saturday to Friday — the same week driver invoices are paid on — so mileage and pay line up on the same days. History has been backfilled and it updates automatically from here.
2Fuel range — miles until empty
Issue
"On the tracker with the fuel range Finder I need a line that clearly states how much gas until they run out with a 15 mile buffer — not seeing that."
Assessment
You were right that it wasn't there. The 15-mile buffer has been in the system since 18 August, but it only appeared inside the route check — which shows up once a load with a route is selected. The always-visible figure was labelled "mi to plan on", which is a planning floor, not a distance to empty. The word "empty" appeared nowhere on the screen.
Fix
The Fuel & Route panel now leads with the number to act on, with the distance to dry underneath — and it shows whether or not a load is selected:
168 mi before you refuel 183 mi to dry  ·  holds back 15 mi reserve
The same figure now appears on the driver's phone and their top-bar fuel chip, so dispatch and the driver can never quote different numbers to each other. A truck already into its reserve reads "Refuel now" rather than a misleadingly small number, and when there is no usable fuel reading the panel says so instead of showing a figure it cannot stand behind.
3Mileage error found while building this
Issue
LogisX-#2372's recorded mileage was badly overstated.
Assessment
Its tracker reset on 11 August and the odometer dropped 219,818 miles in about ten minutes. The old calculation measured a day as highest reading minus lowest, so it counted that reset as distance driven and recorded a single 219,818-mile day. That one row was inflating the truck's all-time total on Fleet Health and on the investor portal.
Fix
Mileage is now measured by adding up actual movement between readings, so a device reset contributes nothing.
#2372 corrected: 244,562 → 24,776 miles Every other truck's figures were unchanged. Two genuinely long days (1,247 and 1,232 miles) were checked and left intact.
