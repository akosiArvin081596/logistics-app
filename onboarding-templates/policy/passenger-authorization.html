Part 3. Rules the passenger must follow

  1. The passenger must never drive the truck, never sit in the driver's seat while the engine is running, and never try to operate the truck in any way.
  2. The passenger must be older than 10.
  3. The passenger must fit the seat and the seat belt properly: sitting all the way back with their back against the seat back, knees bent over the front edge of the seat, the lap part of the belt low across the hips and upper thighs (below the stomach), and the shoulder part resting on the shoulder and the middle of the chest, clear of the neck and face.
  4. The passenger must wear the seat belt the whole time the truck is moving, and may unbuckle only after the truck has come to a full stop and the engine has been turned off.
  5. The driver agrees to make sure the passenger follows these rules.
  6. A copy of this authorization, on paper or on the driver's phone, must be kept in the truck while the passenger is aboard and shown to any law enforcement or safety official who asks to see it.

Part 4. No insurance for passengers

The Company does not offer insurance for passengers, and nothing in this form promises the passenger any insurance coverage. The passenger (or, for a passenger under 18, the parent or legal guardian) is responsible for any insurance the passenger wants or needs.

Part 5. Release of claims

By signing below, the passenger (or, for a passenger under 18, the parent or legal guardian, for the passenger and for themselves) and the driver each agree, to the fullest extent the law allows:

(a) to give up and release every claim they have now or may have later against the Company and its owners, officers, employees, agents and representatives, and anyone acting for the Company, for injury, illness, death, or damage to or loss of property that results from any accident or event while the passenger is riding in, getting into or getting out of any vehicle the Company owns, leases, operates or has under contract, whether or not the claim is known today; and

(b) to hold the Company and those people harmless for those claims, meaning they will not sue them or ask them to pay for them.

Each person signing confirms that they have read this form, understand that they are giving up legal rights, and are signing it of their own free will.
