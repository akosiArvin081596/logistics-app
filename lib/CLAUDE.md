# `lib/` — shared server-side modules

Shared server-side modules live in `lib/` (required from `server.js`):
- `ifta-states.js` — US state bounding-box lookup used by the IFTA mileage classifier.
- `pdf-browser.js` — Puppeteer HTML→PDF renderer for onboarding/investor docs (why `puppeteer` is a top-level dep alongside `pdfkit`/`pdf-lib`).
- `policy-field-maps.js`, `policy-renderer.js` — field mapping + template rendering for onboarding legal documents. `renderPolicy()` is `prepareTemplateHtml()` (the template bytes, the payment-terms slots applied, the logo) plus `buildRenderFields()` (the field map's output, with the payment-terms labels merged into `text` and `requiredText` only when `data.paymentTerms` is set); with no terms both are exactly what the renderer did before.
- `investor-payment-terms.js` — per-investor payment terms for the invitation links (pure, `crypto` only): the input rules (`normalizeTermsInput()`, `parseLeaseAmountToCents()` from the digits, `normalizeDetails()`, `LIMITS`), the token (`newInviteToken()`, `hashInviteToken()`), the frozen snapshot (`snapshotJson()` / `parseSnapshot()`, which throws `PAYMENT_TERMS_SNAPSHOT_INVALID`), `formatMoneyCents()` / `describeTerms()`, and the template slots (`applyPaymentTermsToHtml()`, `renderFieldsFor()`, `TEMPLATE_SLOTS`). A template slot is whole-line markers — `<!-- payment-terms:slot NAME -->`, default lines, `<template data-payment-terms="split|lease">` variants, `<!-- /payment-terms:slot -->` — and every marker line is dropped from the output, so the standard render is the template's original bytes; a malformed, duplicated, missing or stray marker throws `PAYMENT_TERMS_TEMPLATE_INVALID`. Values reach the PDF only through the aria-label fill. A lease amount is whole dollars (`MESSAGES.invalid_amount`); a snapshot signed with cents still reads. ⚠️ **No payout code may read it**: the terms reach payouts only through the `investor_payout_basis` row the acceptance records (`recordSignedPayoutBasis()` in `server.js`). `client/src/lib/paymentTerms.js` mirrors the input rules and labels. `scripts/test-payment-terms-template.js` (hash pins, wording, refusals, case table, timing), `scripts/test-payment-terms-routes.js`, `scripts/test-payment-terms-parity.mjs`.
- `investor-payout-basis.js` — THE payout function (pure, no require): `settleInvestorMonths()` is the split (`Math.round(netProfit * splitFraction)` on the raw net profit, exactly as before) or the fixed monthly lease, then `carryForward()`, the one loss walk (a lease month pays its lease and leaves the split deficit alone). Both monthly builders in `server.js` call it; `computeLossCarryForward()` is a call to `carryForward()`. Also the basis resolution (`governingBasisRow()`, `leaseBasisForMonth()`, `firstPayoutMonth()`; a month finalized as the split stays the split whatever row governs it: `payoutBasisContext()` in `server.js` passes those months as `settledSplitMonths`, read with `frozenBreakdownIsLease()`; a month finalized as a lease pays exactly the `payoutBasis` it froze, flag on or off, whatever the settings or trucks say now: `settledLeaseMonths`, read with `readFrozenLeaseBasis()` and applied by `settledLeaseBasis()`; `isLeaseMonth()` is the load report's month test), the flag gate (`leaseBasisActive()`, the one test of `INVESTOR_LEASE_PAYOUTS_ENABLED`), the idle predicate both builders share (`isZeroActivityMonth()`), coverage and proration (`leaseCoverage()`, `leaseMonthPayment()`; `truckInFleet()` counts a truck in every status but `Inactive`: THE fleet rule, which every fixed-cost, maintenance-fund and compliance-fee site in `server.js` reads too, `truckInFleetSql()` in a query, since 2026-09-30, when a truck in Maintenance or OOS kept its fixed costs by the owner's decision, and `GET /api/investor`'s per-truck projections since 2026-10-01), the three settings (`readLeaseSettings()`) and the admin PUT's input rules (`readBasisInput()`: 400 `INVALID_BASIS` / `LEASE_AMOUNT_WHOLE_DOLLARS`, 409 `BASIS_MONTH_CLOSED`; a start month from `EFFECTIVE_MONTH_MIN`, 2020-01). No investor-facing wording lives here (`lease-payout-text.js`). See [`investor-money-math.md`](../docs/claude/investor-money-math.md). `scripts/test-investor-payout-basis.js`, `scripts/test-payout-basis-routes.js`.
- `lease-payout-text.js` — the investor-facing wording for a month paid as a fixed monthly lease, the agreed sentences L1–L10 verbatim, in their one server home (pure, no require): `LEASE_TEXT` (`LABEL`, `SUB_LINE`, `EXPLAIN`, `REASON`, `LOSS_MONTH`, `REPORT_NOTE` / `_FROM` / `_DURING`, `REPORT_LABEL`, `PER_LOAD_SHARE`). `payout-statement.js` prints L3–L7 from here and `server.js` L10; `investor-report-options.js` loads nothing, so it keeps copies of L8–L9 that `scripts/test-investor-report-options.js` pins equal to these; `client/src/lib/leasePayoutText.js` is the client copy, pinned by `scripts/test-lease-payout-text.mjs`. It is one of the statement template files (`STATEMENT_TEMPLATE_FILES`), so a reworded sentence re-renders the stored statements. ⚠️ Every text is listed in `docs/investor-portal-copy.md` §17.
- `investor-report-options.js` — the downloadable investor report's and tax CSV's choices, one switch or text table each, the one place to change them (pure, no require): `RANGE_MODE` (`"whole-months"` shipped / `"exact-dates"`), `UNPRICED_TRUCKS` (`"not-available"` shipped / `"zero"`, the documents before 2026-09-30), the `NOTE` templates printed under the report's Income Statement, `UNPRICED_TEXT`, `RANGE_HINT` and `DATE_ERRORS`, plus `reportDateRange()` (the 400 `INVALID_DATE` / `INVALID_RANGE` check and the whole-month widening), `truckPriceFigures()` and `fill()`. A switch naming no setting throws at load. `server.js` passes each switch explicitly, and `GET /api/investor` hands `RANGE_MODE` to the client as `reportRangeMode`.
  - **Lease payouts.** The report prints the payout twice, and each label is a `SPLIT` / `LEASE` / `MIXED` table picked by the months the report covers (every month split, every month lease, or both): `PAYOUT_LABEL` for the Income Statement's payout row and `OWNER_EARNINGS_LABEL` for the figure beside Net Cash Flow. ⚠️ Each `SPLIT` is `fill()`ed with `{ pct }` and must print exactly the label the server printed before (`Investor Payout (${splitPctLabel}%)`, `Owner Earnings (${splitPctLabel}%)`), so a split investor's report does not change by a character. `NOTE.LEASE` / `NOTE.LEASE_FROM` / `NOTE.LEASE_DURING` join the note under the Income Statement when every month is a lease / for a lease that runs from a month to the end of the range (`{month}` is its first lease month) / for lease months that a split month follows (`{span}` is those months, so the sentence does not read as running on); `{amount}` is whole dollars (`$2,000`). `PAYOUT_LABEL.LEASE` and the three are copies of `lease-payout-text.js`'s L8–L9, pinned equal by the runner.
  - ⚠️ Every text here reaches an investor and is listed in `docs/investor-portal-copy.md` §16, the lease ones in §17; `client/src/lib/investorReportText.js` copies `RANGE_HINT`. See [`investor-money-math.md`](../docs/claude/investor-money-math.md). `scripts/test-investor-report-options.js`, `scripts/test-investor-report-choices.js`.
- `payout-statement.js` — the investor statement PDF's HTML for one finalized or paid month (`buildPayoutStatementHtml()`). The amount settled is the **frozen** ledger figure; the itemized appendix is a **live recompute**, and so is page 1's composition for a month closed without a snapshot, and the drift note is what reconciles a recompute that has moved. Rendered PDFs are stored and re-served, keyed on a hash of this file and the other template files (`STATEMENT_TEMPLATE_FILES` in `server.js`), so any edit here re-renders every statement on its next download. A month whose `breakdown.payoutBasis` is set (a lease month, live and in the frozen `finalized_breakdown`) prints the lease block instead of the split: `How your payment is calculated`, the monthly lease, a reason line when the month is prorated, idle or has no truck in service, what the month pays, then the truck's revenue and costs as a separate table for information. Its words are `LEASE_TEXT` (exported): the statement's own labels, and L3–L7 taken from `lease-payout-text.js`; all are listed in `docs/investor-portal-copy.md` §17. With no `payoutBasis` the page is the split statement, byte for byte.
  - ⚠️ **Dates: a bare `YYYY-MM-DD` is a calendar date, an ISO timestamp an instant.** `stmtDate()` anchors a bare date (the due date, a load's or an expense's date) at 12:00 UTC before `formatDate()` prints its Houston date, so it prints as itself under any server clock; an instant (`paid_at`, `finalized_at`, `adjusted_at`) prints its Houston date. Never parse a bare date at local midnight: on the UTC production server that printed every one a day early until 2026-09-30.
  - `scripts/test-payout-statement-lease.js` pins eight split pages under the Houston clock: seven are 61406a1's; the loss page is 61406a1's less the false change note (a losing month now foots to its settled $0 with the carried loss shown). It renders every date under five clocks, each in its own process.
- `routemate-client.js` — Routemate ELD/telematics API adapter (single point of contact).
- `linxup-push.js` — Linxup Push API v3 message mapping (pure, no network/DB): `detectMessageType()`, `normalizePosition()` (→ neutral telemetry shape), `vehicleIdCandidates()`, `speedToMps()`. server.js owns the webhook, token gate, DB insert, and geofence fan-out. See [`linxup.md`](../docs/claude/linxup.md).
- `ratecon-load.js` — pure helpers for rate-con ingestion, now shared by **both** paths: `buildJobTrackingRow()` (header-order mapping + creation defaults), `calculateRatePerMile()` / `cityStateZip()` (ported from the n8n `Calculate RPM + Details` node — never "Calculate Rate Per Mile", which does not exist; the n8n original has since been deleted as unreachable), `extractionWarnings()`, `upsert`-support helpers. No network or DB access. Called by `POST /api/loads/from-ratecon` **and** by `POST /api/n8n/load-distance`, which is what makes the email path and the drag-and-drop path agree. See "Load ingestion" in the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md).
  - **⚠️ `cityStateZip()` normalizes newlines to commas first, and that line is load-bearing.** Its character class was `[^,]`, which matches `\n`, so a multi-line rate-con address (`"4528 W Royal Ln\nIrving, TX 75063"` — the common shape) had its whole street swallowed into the city capture, and `Details` read `"4528 W Royal Ln\nIrving, TX 75063 - 818 Hallmark Dr\nLAREDO, TX 78045"`. Measured on the 144 production rows where the retired LLM had also answered: **118/144 (81.9%) before, 140/144 (97.2%) after**. This was the **precondition** for retiring the LLM, not a tidy-up — moving the address half onto a parser that leaks streets would have swapped a wrong distance for a wrong address. `node scripts/check-ratecon-address-parsing.js` locks it (16 cases, all verbatim production addresses, exits 1 on failure). Known residual, deliberately not chased: an address with no delimiter between street and city keeps the street — 1 of 144, and every rule that would split them guesses where a street name ends.
- `scankit-client.js` — ScanKit.io document-scanning API adapter (single point of contact; `POST /scan/crop`). See the AI / vision services in [`backend-server.md`](../docs/claude/backend-server.md#ai--vision-services).
- **Also in `lib/` and NOT detailed above** — `ls lib/` lists every module; these are the ones this file does not walk through, largest first: `ratecon-reconcile.js` (45 KB — `reconcileRateCons()`, the out-of-band catch for the ingestion blind spot described under "Load ingestion"; referenced in prose there but easy to miss as a module), `expense-analytics.js` (26 KB), `expense-ai.js` (23 KB), `ratecon-drive-index.js` (21 KB — the load→rate-con Drive linking behind `POST /api/admin/ratecon-index`), `receipt-ocr.js`, `pii-mask.js` (the masking-on-read layer from [`pii-at-rest.md`](../docs/claude/pii-at-rest.md)), `receipt-duplicates.js`, `gemini-errors.js`, `csv.js`.
- `broker-invoice.js` — invoice assembly for **every** broker: `resolveInvoiceTo()` / `resolveBrokerName()` recipient + naming, `isBisonLoad()` (now a recipient *branch*, not a gate), deterministic rate-con field extraction, and the payload shape for the Draft Invoice route. The email builders: `buildInvoiceEmailHtml()` (the Gmail draft body; takes an edited message as `bodyText`), `buildInvoiceEmailBodyText()` (the generated message as plain text, the editor's starting text, served as `emailBodyDefault`) and `buildInvoiceEmailSignatureHtml()` (the fixed signature and confidentiality footer, never editable, served as `emailSignatureHtml`); see [`invoice-drafting.md`](../docs/claude/invoice-drafting.md). See "Invoice drafting — all brokers" in the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md).
  - ⚠️ **`formatDate()` reads a day as text, and converts only an instant.** A bare `YYYY-MM-DD` or a zone-less `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM[:SS]` prints as the day it names (no day lost — `new Date()` would read it as UTC midnight, the day before in Houston); a `Z` / offset / RFC-2822 instant prints the Houston day it fell on; an impossible date (`2026-02-31`) prints blank. `scripts/test-invoice-overrides.js` pins each case.
- `imap-draft.js` — `appendGmailDraft()`: writes an assembled invoice email straight into the Gmail Drafts folder over IMAP (no send), using `GMAIL_USER` / `GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD`.
- `receipt-gallons-recovery.js` — pure decision logic for recovering GALLONS onto fuel receipts that have a price and no volume: `cpgBand()`, `judgeGallonsRecovery()`, `summarizeRecovery()`. No network/DB/fs. See "Gallons recovery" in [`fuel.md`](../docs/claude/fuel.md).
- `eld-miles.js` — pure odometer→miles math (no network/DB): `acceptOdoDelta()`, `sumOdoDeltas()`, `splitDeltasByDayAndDriver()`, `dayBasis()`, `basisLabel()`, plus the canonical `usTzForLongitude()` / `localDayInTz()` and `buildDriverAtResolver()`. **⚠️ Miles are the SUM OF POSITIVE CONSECUTIVE DELTAS, never `MAX(odo) − MIN(odo)`** — an ELD reset on `LogisX-#2372` (2026-08-11) made the span bill 219,818 miles as distance for one day. Guards are per-delta and there is **deliberately no per-day cap**: a real 1,247-mile day exists in this data. A rejected delta is **dropped, never carried** — carrying it re-injects the outlier one row later. Powers `GET /api/analytics/mileage` and the corrected `rollupOneDay()`.
- `load-haul.js` — pure per-LOAD haul measurement (no network/DB; deps are geolib + `eld-miles`): `resolveHaulWindow()`, `computeHaulMiles()`, `legMiles()`, `deadheadFloorMs()`, `buildTruckAtResolver()`, `windowsOverlap()`. **⚠️ The window is REPLAYED FROM PINGS, never read off `load_status_history` — `changed_at` is when the driver TAPPED A BUTTON.** Load `564157463`'s whole lifecycle spans **1 m 54 s**; a status-window sum reports **0.0 miles** for a truck that drove 248 that day, and the In Transit→Delivered span has a **2.4-minute minimum** across the 42 loads carrying both markers. So departure/arrival come from `geolib.isPointWithinRadius` against `load_coordinates`, mirroring the live geofence — validated on `563166022`, the one load with a fully geofence-derived window: replayed departure **18:33:33Z** against the real geofence row's **18:34:17Z**. ⚠️ `effectiveRadiusM()` shrinks the zones on a lane shorter than twice the radius, the same geometry that would otherwise strand a short load at "At Shipper". ⚠️ `buildTruckAtResolver()` is the HISTORICAL driver→truck question and is **not** `resolveTruckForDriverName()`, which answers "right now" and would measure last month's haul off this month's truck.
- `fuel-model.js` — pure fuel-range math (no network/DB): `computeRange()`, `deriveMpg()` (from ELD odometer/fuel deltas, guards refuels/tiny samples), `hasFuelSensor()` (distinguishes "no fuel sensor" from an empty tank), `estimateRangeForVehicle()`. Defaults `DEFAULT_TANK_GALLONS=200`, `DEFAULT_MPG=6.5`. Powers `GET /api/fuel/range`. Also holds the **range verification + trip-planning** math — `measureBurnRate()`, `backtestRangeFormula()`, `rangeInterval()`, `planTripFuel()`, `tripReserveMiles()`. See "Fuel range honesty" in [`fuel.md`](../docs/claude/fuel.md).
- `poi-fuel-stops.js` — `findFuelStopsAlongRoute({originLat,originLng,destLat,destLng,apiKey,fetchImpl,limit})` → diesel truck stops along a route via Google Places (New) `places:searchNearby` (`includedTypes:['truck_stop','gas_station']`) at sampled waypoints; dedupes by placeId, ranks truck-stop/known-diesel-brand first, tags `aboutMilesFromRoute`. The field mask includes `places.fuelOptions`, so each stop carries a **live per-station diesel pump price** (`extractDiesel()` prefers `TRUCK_DIESEL` then `DIESEL`; `dieselPrice`/`dieselType`/`dieselPriceUpdated`, null where Google has no coverage — ~⅔ of major-chain stops do). Priced stations get a rank boost so a cheap stop survives the result cap. Powers `GET /api/poi/fuel-stops` (rate-limited by `poiLimiter`).
- `ratecon-normalize.js` — pure post-extraction hardening for multi-broker rate-cons: `normalizeRateConFields()` (money→`$X,XXX.XX`, collapse multi-line addresses, tidy phones, `None`/`""`→null), `missingCriticalFields()`, `addressLooksUsable()` / `unusableCriticalFields()`, `mergeExtractions()`. Wired into `POST /api/loads/ratecon/extract` **and, since 2026-08-09, `POST /api/n8n/extract-pdf-via-gemini`** with a second-pass Gemini nudge when a critical field is missing — see "Unusable addresses" in [`load-ingestion.md`](../docs/claude/load-ingestion.md) for why the unattended half went 151 loads without it. Paired with the hardened `RATECON_PDF_SYSTEM_PROMPT` (multi-stop first-pickup/last-drop, total-pay vs line-haul/accessorial, agent-vs-brokerage, appointment windows, ref disambiguation, injection resistance).
- **Added 2026-09-23** (one module per change, each with its own runner):
  - `eld-feed-health.js` — pure decisions for the hourly ELD feed-silence sweep (a stale, never-reported, trickling or orphaned feed), behind the `eld_feed_alerts` ledger and `ELD_STALE_ALERT_ENABLED` (a kill switch — default ON). A silent feed quietly moves a truck onto the estimated pay basis, which is why this is an alarm and not a dashboard. It also holds the alert pacing: a resolved feed that re-opens may alert again only 24 h after its last alert (`reopenCooldownActive()`), and the daily cap counts delivered sends in a rolling window (`sendsInWindow()`). Every comparison is in epoch milliseconds (`stampMs()`) — never ISO text against SQLite's `datetime()` format. `scripts/test-eld-feed-staleness.js`.
  - `expense-window.js` — the one definition of which loads a driver may still add a receipt to: an active load, or one delivered within the last 7 × 24 h (timed from `load_status_history`). Its two callers must never disagree: `GET /api/driver/:driverName` ships the verdict as `_expenseWindow`, and `POST /api/expenses` enforces it for the Driver role. `scripts/test-expense-load-window.js`.
  - `image-size.js` — image type and dimensions read from the file header, without decoding, plus the limits every in-process decode in `server.js` is held to (`checkImage()`, `LIMITS`). A refusal is 413 `IMAGE_TOO_LARGE` or 415 `UNSUPPORTED_IMAGE_TYPE`, and an image whose dimensions cannot be read is refused too. `scripts/test-image-limits.js`.
    - `servedType(buf, { pdf })` is what stored bytes may be SERVED as, from their magic bytes and never from a stored label: `image/jpeg`, `image/png` or `image/webp`, `application/pdf` only with `{ pdf: true }` and a `%PDF-` start, otherwise `null`. `storedFileForServing()` in `server.js` serves a driver's truck photo and identity files through it (404 when `null`).
    - `LIMITS.TRUCK_PHOTO` (JPEG, PNG or WebP, at most 16 MP and 2 MiB, both enforced by `checkImage()`) holds the truck photo writes. First, `TRUCK_PHOTO_DATA_URI_MAX_LENGTH` is checked on the string before anything is decoded: the 2 MiB as base64, plus room for a prefix. `TRUCK_PHOTO_MAX_BYTES` and that length are both derived from the `LIMITS` entry, the one copy of the number. `truckPhotoForStorage()` in `server.js` stores a photo that passes in canonical form: `data:<servedType()>;base64,<the bytes re-encoded>`. `refusalBody(verdict, "truckPhoto")` words the refusals: "This photo is too large. Use a smaller JPEG, PNG or WebP image." (413) / "This photo could not be read. Use a JPEG, PNG or WebP image." (415). `scripts/test-stored-file-serving.js`.
  - `public-form-input.js` — the boundary checks for the unauthenticated public forms (`checkPublicScalars()`, `checkPublicEmail()`, `checkPublicVehicles()`). Pure and total (never throws), and the only implementation: every public route calls it rather than a hand-copied rule. `scripts/test-public-form-input.js`.
  - `w9-input.js` — what the investor W-9 prints, checked before it is stored or rendered: `checkW9Tin()` (nine digits, hyphens and spaces only, 400 `INVALID_TIN`), and `checkW9Printable()` / `checkW9Text()` (400 `UNSUPPORTED_CHARACTERS`), which ask pdf-lib's own standard Helvetica and Helvetica Bold, the fonts `fillW9Form()` fills with, whether they can encode the text, one code point at a time. ⚠️ Never replace that with a character list: the answer must be the fill's. ⚠️ The length caps (`W9_TEXT_MAX_LENGTH`: legal name and DBA 200, address 300; `W9_SIGNATURE_MAX_LENGTH` 200; 400 `VALUE_TOO_LONG`) are checked BEFORE that walk and must stay before it: the walk is per character, and the routes that call it are anonymous. It takes strings only (anything else is 400 `INVALID_FIELD`, in `public-form-input.js`'s words, copied as `INVALID_VALUE_MESSAGE` and pinned equal), because the fill splits the address and draws the signature as text. ⚠️ It has no relative `require`, and must keep none: two runners load it with their own `require`. Pure and total. `client/src/lib/taxId.js` is the client's copy of the TIN rule. `scripts/test-w9-input-checks.js`, which also pins `fillW9Form()`'s failure causes.
  - `tesseract-ocr-child.js` — one receipt-OCR job in its own process, started only by `runReceiptOcrChild()` in `server.js`; anything that goes wrong ends the child, never the server. `scripts/test-receipt-ocr-crash.js`.
